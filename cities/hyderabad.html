Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Hyderabad?

Over the last ten years Hyderabad averaged a daytime high of 31.2°C and a nighttime low of 21.2°C, with about 8884 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was May and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Hyderabad?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is May at 31.2°C, and the coldest is December at 21.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Hyderabad?

Snow is rare in Hyderabad: the coldest average nights only drop to about 21.2°C, which is above freezing.
