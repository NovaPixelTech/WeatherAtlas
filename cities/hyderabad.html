Seasons in Hyderabad

Hyderabad is warmest in May, when average highs reach 37.1°C, and coolest in December at 28.2°C. Rainfall peaks in July (219.2 mm) and is lowest in February (2.6 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Hyderabad

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 31.3°C and about 2.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is May and the coolest is December; the driest month is February and the wettest is July.

How Hyderabad compares

With an annual mean of 26.2°C and about 888.4 mm of rain, Hyderabad has consistently warm climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Hyderabad?

Over the last ten years Hyderabad averaged a daytime high of 31.2°C and a nighttime low of 21.2°C, with about 888.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was May and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Hyderabad?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is May at 37.1°C, and the coolest is December at 28.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Hyderabad?

Snow is rare in Hyderabad: even the coldest average nights stay around 17°C, above freezing.
