Best time to visit Buenos Aires

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18°C and about 679.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is June and the wettest is March.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Buenos Aires?

Over the last ten years Buenos Aires averaged a daytime high of 21.2°C and a nighttime low of 14°C, with about 9604.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Buenos Aires?

The driest month is June and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is January at 21.2°C, and the coldest is July at 14°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Buenos Aires?

Snow is rare in Buenos Aires: the coldest average nights only drop to about 14°C, which is above freezing.
