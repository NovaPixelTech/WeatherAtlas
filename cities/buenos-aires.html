Seasons in Buenos Aires

Buenos Aires is warmest in January, when average highs reach 28.3°C, and coolest in July at 13.9°C. Rainfall peaks in March (118.4 mm) and is lowest in June (49.5 mm).

February, March, April, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Buenos Aires

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18°C and about 67.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is June and the wettest is March.

How Buenos Aires compares

With an annual mean of 17.6°C and about 960.5 mm of rain, Buenos Aires has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in March.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Buenos Aires?

Over the last ten years Buenos Aires averaged a daytime high of 21.2°C and a nighttime low of 14°C, with about 960.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Buenos Aires?

The driest month is June and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is January at 28.3°C, and the coolest is July at 13.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Buenos Aires?

Snow is rare in Buenos Aires: even the coldest average nights stay around 7.5°C, above freezing.
