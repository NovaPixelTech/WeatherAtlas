Seasons in Tbilisi

Tbilisi is warmest in August, when average highs reach 31.6°C, and coolest in January at 7.2°C. Rainfall peaks in May (97.1 mm) and is lowest in January (19.1 mm).

April, May, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Tbilisi

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 26.1°C and about 53.4 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is May.

How Tbilisi compares

With an annual mean of 14.1°C and about 591.8 mm of rain, Tbilisi has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tbilisi?

Over the last ten years Tbilisi averaged a daytime high of 19°C and a nighttime low of 9.2°C, with about 591.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tbilisi?

The driest month is January and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is August at 31.6°C, and the coolest is January at 7.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tbilisi?

Snow is possible in Tbilisi: the coldest average nights drop to around -1.4°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
