Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tbilisi?

Over the last ten years Tbilisi averaged a daytime high of 19°C and a nighttime low of 9.2°C, with about 5918 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tbilisi?

The driest month is January and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is August at 19°C, and the coldest is January at 9.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tbilisi?

Snowfall is part of Tbilisi's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 9.2°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
