Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Cairo?

Over the last ten years Cairo averaged a daytime high of 29.6°C and a nighttime low of 17°C, with about 359.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Cairo?

The driest month is August and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is July at 29.6°C, and the coldest is January at 17°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Cairo?

Snow is rare in Cairo: the coldest average nights only drop to about 17°C, which is above freezing.
