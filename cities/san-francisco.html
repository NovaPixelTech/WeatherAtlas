Seasons in San Francisco

San Francisco is warmest in September, when average highs reach 21.2°C, and coolest in December at 13.5°C. Rainfall peaks in December (128.6 mm) and is lowest in August (0.6 mm).

June, July, August, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit San Francisco

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 20.1°C and about 0.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is September and the coolest is December; the driest month is August and the wettest is December.

How San Francisco compares

With an annual mean of 14.1°C and about 580.7 mm of rain, San Francisco has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in San Francisco?

Over the last ten years San Francisco averaged a daytime high of 17.3°C and a nighttime low of 10.9°C, with about 580.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit San Francisco?

The driest month is August and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is September at 21.2°C, and the coolest is December at 13.5°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in San Francisco?

Snow is rare in San Francisco: even the coldest average nights stay around 7.7°C, above freezing.
