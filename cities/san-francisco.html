Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in San Francisco?

Over the last ten years San Francisco averaged a daytime high of 17.3°C and a nighttime low of 10.9°C, with about 5807.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit San Francisco?

The driest month is August and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is September at 17.3°C, and the coldest is December at 10.9°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in San Francisco?

Snow is rare in San Francisco: the coldest average nights only drop to about 10.9°C, which is above freezing.
