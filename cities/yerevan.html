Seasons in Yerevan

Yerevan is warmest in August, when average highs reach 33.7°C, and coolest in January at 3.4°C. Rainfall peaks in May (84.3 mm) and is lowest in August (8.2 mm).

April, May, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Yerevan

Based on long-term averages, October is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.9°C and about 39.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is August and the wettest is May.

How Yerevan compares

With an annual mean of 13.5°C and about 427.2 mm of rain, Yerevan has moderate climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on October, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Yerevan?

Over the last ten years Yerevan averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 8.1°C, with about 427.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Yerevan?

The driest month is August and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is August at 33.7°C, and the coolest is January at 3.4°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Yerevan?

Snow is possible in Yerevan: the coldest average nights drop to around -4.6°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
