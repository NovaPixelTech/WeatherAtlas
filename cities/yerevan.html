Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Yerevan?

Over the last ten years Yerevan averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 8.1°C, with about 4271.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Yerevan?

The driest month is August and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is August at 18.9°C, and the coldest is January at 8.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Yerevan?

Snowfall is part of Yerevan's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 8.1°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
