Seasons in Taipei

Taipei is warmest in July, when average highs reach 32.7°C, and coolest in January at 18.9°C. Rainfall peaks in June (250.5 mm) and is lowest in January (68 mm).

January, February, March, April, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Taipei

Based on long-term averages, January is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18.9°C and about 68 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is June.

How Taipei compares

With an annual mean of 23°C and about 1803 mm of rain, Taipei has generally mild to warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on January, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Taipei?

Over the last ten years Taipei averaged a daytime high of 26°C and a nighttime low of 19.9°C, with about 1803 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Taipei?

The driest month is January and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is July at 32.7°C, and the coolest is January at 18.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Taipei?

Snow is rare in Taipei: even the coldest average nights stay around 13.5°C, above freezing.
