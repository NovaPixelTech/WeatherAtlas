Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Montreal?

Over the last ten years Montreal averaged a daytime high of 10.7°C and a nighttime low of 1.9°C, with about 11442.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Montreal?

The driest month is February and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 10.7°C, and the coldest is January at 1.9°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Montreal?

Snowfall is part of Montreal's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 1.9°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
