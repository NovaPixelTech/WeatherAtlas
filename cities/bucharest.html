Seasons in Bucharest

Bucharest is warmest in August, when average highs reach 30.4°C, and coolest in January at 3.9°C. Rainfall peaks in June (80.7 mm) and is lowest in February (30.7 mm).

May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Bucharest

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.3°C and about 40.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is June.

How Bucharest compares

With an annual mean of 12.9°C and about 630.6 mm of rain, Bucharest has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bucharest?

Over the last ten years Bucharest averaged a daytime high of 17.8°C and a nighttime low of 8°C, with about 630.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Bucharest?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 30.4°C, and the coolest is January at 3.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bucharest?

Snow is possible in Bucharest: the coldest average nights drop to around -3.9°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
