Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bucharest?

Over the last ten years Bucharest averaged a daytime high of 17.8°C and a nighttime low of 8°C, with about 6305.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Bucharest?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 17.8°C, and the coldest is January at 8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bucharest?

Snowfall is part of Bucharest's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 8°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
