Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Reykjavik?

Over the last ten years Reykjavik averaged a daytime high of 6.9°C and a nighttime low of 2.2°C, with about 15322.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Reykjavik?

The driest month is July and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 6.9°C, and the coldest is January at 2.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Reykjavik?

Snowfall is part of Reykjavik's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 2.2°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
