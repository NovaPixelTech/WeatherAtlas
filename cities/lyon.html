Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Lyon?

Over the last ten years Lyon averaged a daytime high of 17.7°C and a nighttime low of 8.5°C, with about 8711.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Lyon?

The driest month is February and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 17.7°C, and the coldest is January at 8.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Lyon?

Snow is rare in Lyon: the coldest average nights only drop to about 8.5°C, which is above freezing.
