Seasons in Lyon

Lyon is warmest in July, when average highs reach 28.6°C, and coolest in January at 7°C. Rainfall peaks in October (98.6 mm) and is lowest in February (44.4 mm).

May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Lyon

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 23.6°C and about 70.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is October.

How Lyon compares

With an annual mean of 13.1°C and about 871.1 mm of rain, Lyon has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in October.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Lyon?

Over the last ten years Lyon averaged a daytime high of 17.7°C and a nighttime low of 8.5°C, with about 871.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Lyon?

The driest month is February and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 28.6°C, and the coolest is January at 7°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Lyon?

Snow is rare in Lyon: even the coldest average nights stay around 0.7°C, above freezing.
