Seasons in Ankara

Ankara is warmest in August, when average highs reach 31.9°C, and coolest in January at 5.5°C. Rainfall peaks in January (59.5 mm) and is lowest in July (8.1 mm).

April, May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Ankara

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.4°C and about 13.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How Ankara compares

With an annual mean of 13.2°C and about 436.2 mm of rain, Ankara has moderate climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Ankara?

Over the last ten years Ankara averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 7.4°C, with about 436.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Ankara?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 31.9°C, and the coolest is January at 5.5°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Ankara?

Snow is possible in Ankara: the coldest average nights drop to around -3.1°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
