Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Ankara?

Over the last ten years Ankara averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 7.4°C, with about 4362.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Ankara?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 18.9°C, and the coldest is January at 7.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Ankara?

Snowfall is part of Ankara's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 7.4°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
