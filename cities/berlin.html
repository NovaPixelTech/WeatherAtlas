Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Berlin?

Over the last ten years Berlin averaged a daytime high of 14.8°C and a nighttime low of 6.8°C, with about 6034.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Berlin?

The driest month is April and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 14.8°C, and the coldest is January at 6.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Berlin?

Snowfall is part of Berlin's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 6.8°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
