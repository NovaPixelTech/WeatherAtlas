Seasons in Melbourne

Melbourne is warmest in January, when average highs reach 26.5°C, and coolest in July at 13.2°C. Rainfall peaks in November (80.9 mm) and is lowest in February (31.3 mm).

January, February, March, April, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Melbourne

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.6°C and about 31.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is February and the wettest is November.

How Melbourne compares

With an annual mean of 15°C and about 739.1 mm of rain, Melbourne has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Melbourne?

Over the last ten years Melbourne averaged a daytime high of 19.7°C and a nighttime low of 10.3°C, with about 739.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Melbourne?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is January at 26.5°C, and the coolest is July at 13.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Melbourne?

Snow is rare in Melbourne: even the coldest average nights stay around 6.4°C, above freezing.
