Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Mumbai?

Over the last ten years Mumbai averaged a daytime high of 30.5°C and a nighttime low of 23.5°C, with about 22659.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was August.

When is the best time of year to visit Mumbai?

The driest month is April and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 30.5°C, and the coldest is August at 23.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Mumbai?

Snow is rare in Mumbai: the coldest average nights only drop to about 23.5°C, which is above freezing.
