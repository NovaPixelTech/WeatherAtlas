Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Beijing?

Over the last ten years Beijing averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 7.8°C, with about 6281.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was June and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Beijing?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is June at 18.9°C, and the coldest is January at 7.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Beijing?

Snowfall is part of Beijing's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 7.8°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
