Seasons in Beijing

Beijing is warmest in June, when average highs reach 32.6°C, and coolest in January at 2.5°C. Rainfall peaks in July (233.4 mm) and is lowest in January (1.6 mm).

April, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Beijing

Based on long-term averages, April is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.1°C and about 19.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is June and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is July.

How Beijing compares

With an annual mean of 13.4°C and about 628.1 mm of rain, Beijing has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on April, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Beijing?

Over the last ten years Beijing averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 7.8°C, with about 628.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was June and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Beijing?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is June at 32.6°C, and the coolest is January at 2.5°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Beijing?

Snow is possible in Beijing: the coldest average nights drop to around -7°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
