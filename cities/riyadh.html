Seasons in Riyadh

Riyadh is warmest in July, when average highs reach 43.5°C, and coolest in January at 21.1°C. Rainfall peaks in January (14.7 mm) and is lowest in June (0 mm).

January, February, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Riyadh

Based on long-term averages, December is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.2°C and about 10 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is June and the wettest is January.

How Riyadh compares

With an annual mean of 26.7°C and about 77 mm of rain, Riyadh has consistently warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on December, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Riyadh?

Over the last ten years Riyadh averaged a daytime high of 33.2°C and a nighttime low of 20.1°C, with about 77 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Riyadh?

The driest month is June and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is July at 43.5°C, and the coolest is January at 21.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Riyadh?

Snow is rare in Riyadh: even the coldest average nights stay around 8.8°C, above freezing.
