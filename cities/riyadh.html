Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Riyadh?

Over the last ten years Riyadh averaged a daytime high of 33.2°C and a nighttime low of 20.1°C, with about 769.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Riyadh?

The driest month is June and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is July at 33.2°C, and the coldest is January at 20.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Riyadh?

Snow is rare in Riyadh: the coldest average nights only drop to about 20.1°C, which is above freezing.
