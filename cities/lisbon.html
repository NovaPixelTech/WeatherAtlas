Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Lisbon?

Over the last ten years Lisbon averaged a daytime high of 21.2°C and a nighttime low of 13.6°C, with about 5429.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Lisbon?

The driest month is August and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is August at 21.2°C, and the coldest is January at 13.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Lisbon?

Snow is rare in Lisbon: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.6°C, which is above freezing.
