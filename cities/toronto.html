Seasons in Toronto

Toronto is warmest in July, when average highs reach 26.2°C, and coolest in January at -0.1°C. Rainfall peaks in April (100.2 mm) and is lowest in September (59.5 mm).

June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Toronto

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.2°C and about 59.5 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is September and the wettest is April.

How Toronto compares

With an annual mean of 9.1°C and about 881.9 mm of rain, Toronto has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in April.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Toronto?

Over the last ten years Toronto averaged a daytime high of 13.1°C and a nighttime low of 5.1°C, with about 881.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Toronto?

The driest month is September and the wettest is April. The hottest average high is July at 26.2°C, and the coolest is January at -0.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Toronto?

Snow is possible in Toronto: the coldest average nights drop to around -7.3°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
