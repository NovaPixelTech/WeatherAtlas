Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Pune?

Over the last ten years Pune averaged a daytime high of 30.6°C and a nighttime low of 20°C, with about 10963.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Pune?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 30.6°C, and the coldest is July at 20°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Pune?

Snow is rare in Pune: the coldest average nights only drop to about 20°C, which is above freezing.
