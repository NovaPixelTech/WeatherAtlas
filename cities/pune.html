Seasons in Pune

Pune is warmest in April, when average highs reach 37.4°C, and coolest in July at 26.6°C. Rainfall peaks in July (281.5 mm) and is lowest in January (2.6 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Pune

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 26.7°C and about 205.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is April and the coolest is July; the driest month is January and the wettest is July.

How Pune compares

With an annual mean of 25.3°C and about 1096.4 mm of rain, Pune has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Pune?

Over the last ten years Pune averaged a daytime high of 30.6°C and a nighttime low of 20°C, with about 1096.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Pune?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 37.4°C, and the coolest is July at 26.6°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Pune?

Snow is rare in Pune: even the coldest average nights stay around 22.3°C, above freezing.
