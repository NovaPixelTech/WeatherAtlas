Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Sydney?

Over the last ten years Sydney averaged a daytime high of 21.9°C and a nighttime low of 13.7°C, with about 9763.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit Sydney?

The driest month is September and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is January at 21.9°C, and the coldest is June at 13.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Sydney?

Snow is rare in Sydney: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.7°C, which is above freezing.
