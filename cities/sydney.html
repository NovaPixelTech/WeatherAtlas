Seasons in Sydney

Sydney is warmest in January, when average highs reach 26.6°C, and coolest in June at 16.8°C. Rainfall peaks in March (151.5 mm) and is lowest in September (48.6 mm).

January, February, April, May, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Sydney

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 20.6°C and about 48.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is June; the driest month is September and the wettest is March.

How Sydney compares

With an annual mean of 17.8°C and about 976.4 mm of rain, Sydney has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in March.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Sydney?

Over the last ten years Sydney averaged a daytime high of 21.9°C and a nighttime low of 13.7°C, with about 976.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit Sydney?

The driest month is September and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is January at 26.6°C, and the coolest is June at 16.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Sydney?

Snow is rare in Sydney: even the coldest average nights stay around 9°C, above freezing.
