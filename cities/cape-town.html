Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Cape Town?

Over the last ten years Cape Town averaged a daytime high of 21°C and a nighttime low of 13.4°C, with about 6001.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Cape Town?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is January at 21°C, and the coldest is July at 13.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Cape Town?

Snow is rare in Cape Town: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.4°C, which is above freezing.
