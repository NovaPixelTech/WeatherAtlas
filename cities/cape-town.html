Seasons in Cape Town

Cape Town is warmest in January, when average highs reach 25.9°C, and coolest in July at 16.4°C. Rainfall peaks in June (123.7 mm) and is lowest in February (9.7 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Cape Town

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.8°C and about 9.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is February and the wettest is June.

How Cape Town compares

With an annual mean of 17.2°C and about 600.1 mm of rain, Cape Town has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Cape Town?

Over the last ten years Cape Town averaged a daytime high of 21°C and a nighttime low of 13.4°C, with about 600.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Cape Town?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is January at 25.9°C, and the coolest is July at 16.4°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Cape Town?

Snow is rare in Cape Town: even the coldest average nights stay around 9.6°C, above freezing.
