Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Islamabad?

Over the last ten years Islamabad averaged a daytime high of 26.4°C and a nighttime low of 17.1°C, with about 10893.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was June and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Islamabad?

The driest month is December and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is June at 26.4°C, and the coldest is January at 17.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Islamabad?

Snow is rare in Islamabad: the coldest average nights only drop to about 17.1°C, which is above freezing.
