Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tokyo?

Over the last ten years Tokyo averaged a daytime high of 20°C and a nighttime low of 11.7°C, with about 16153.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tokyo?

The driest month is February and the wettest is September. The hottest average high is August at 20°C, and the coldest is January at 11.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tokyo?

Snowfall is part of Tokyo's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 11.7°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
