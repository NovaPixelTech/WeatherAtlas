Seasons in Quito

Quito is warmest in September, when average highs reach 19.6°C, and coolest in January at 17.1°C. Rainfall peaks in March (512.8 mm) and is lowest in August (71.9 mm).

August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Quito

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.2°C and about 71.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is September and the coolest is January; the driest month is August and the wettest is March.

How Quito compares

With an annual mean of 13.2°C and about 3427 mm of rain, Quito has moderate climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in March.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Quito?

Over the last ten years Quito averaged a daytime high of 17.8°C and a nighttime low of 8.5°C, with about 3427 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Quito?

The driest month is August and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is September at 19.6°C, and the coolest is January at 17.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Quito?

Snow is rare in Quito: even the coldest average nights stay around 8.8°C, above freezing.
