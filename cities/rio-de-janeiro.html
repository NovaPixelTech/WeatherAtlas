Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Rio de Janeiro?

Over the last ten years Rio de Janeiro averaged a daytime high of 27.3°C and a nighttime low of 20.9°C, with about 11932.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Rio de Janeiro?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is January at 27.3°C, and the coldest is July at 20.9°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro?

Snow is rare in Rio de Janeiro: the coldest average nights only drop to about 20.9°C, which is above freezing.
