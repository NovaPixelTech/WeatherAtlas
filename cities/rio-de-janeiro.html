Seasons in Rio de Janeiro

Rio de Janeiro is warmest in January, when average highs reach 30.5°C, and coolest in July at 25°C. Rainfall peaks in January (160.6 mm) and is lowest in July (40.3 mm).

April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Rio de Janeiro

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25°C and about 40.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How Rio de Janeiro compares

With an annual mean of 24.1°C and about 1193.3 mm of rain, Rio de Janeiro has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Rio de Janeiro?

Over the last ten years Rio de Janeiro averaged a daytime high of 27.3°C and a nighttime low of 20.9°C, with about 1193.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Rio de Janeiro?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is January at 30.5°C, and the coolest is July at 25°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Rio de Janeiro?

Snow is rare in Rio de Janeiro: even the coldest average nights stay around 18°C, above freezing.
