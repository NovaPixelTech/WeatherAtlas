Seasons in Istanbul

Istanbul is warmest in August, when average highs reach 29.3°C, and coolest in January at 9.1°C. Rainfall peaks in January (107.4 mm) and is lowest in August (15.2 mm).

May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Istanbul

Based on long-term averages, June is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.9°C and about 42.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is August and the wettest is January.

How Istanbul compares

With an annual mean of 15.7°C and about 723.6 mm of rain, Istanbul has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on June, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Istanbul?

Over the last ten years Istanbul averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 12.4°C, with about 723.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Istanbul?

The driest month is August and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 29.3°C, and the coolest is January at 9.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Istanbul?

Snow is rare in Istanbul: even the coldest average nights stay around 4°C, above freezing.
