Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Istanbul?

Over the last ten years Istanbul averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 12.4°C, with about 7235.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Istanbul?

The driest month is August and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 18.9°C, and the coldest is January at 12.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Istanbul?

Snow is rare in Istanbul: the coldest average nights only drop to about 12.4°C, which is above freezing.
