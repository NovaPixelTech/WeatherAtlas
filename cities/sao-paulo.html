Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in São Paulo?

Over the last ten years São Paulo averaged a daytime high of 24.9°C and a nighttime low of 15.6°C, with about 12532.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit São Paulo?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is January at 24.9°C, and the coldest is June at 15.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in São Paulo?

Snow is rare in São Paulo: the coldest average nights only drop to about 15.6°C, which is above freezing.
