Seasons in São Paulo

São Paulo is warmest in January, when average highs reach 27.2°C, and coolest in June at 21.9°C. Rainfall peaks in January (223.8 mm) and is lowest in July (31.4 mm).

March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit São Paulo

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.3°C and about 31.4 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is June; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How São Paulo compares

With an annual mean of 20.3°C and about 1253.2 mm of rain, São Paulo has generally mild to warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in São Paulo?

Over the last ten years São Paulo averaged a daytime high of 24.9°C and a nighttime low of 15.6°C, with about 1253.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit São Paulo?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is January at 27.2°C, and the coolest is June at 21.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in São Paulo?

Snow is rare in São Paulo: even the coldest average nights stay around 12.6°C, above freezing.
