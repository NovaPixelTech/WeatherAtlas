Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Delhi?

Over the last ten years Delhi averaged a daytime high of 30.4°C and a nighttime low of 19.1°C, with about 7517.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was May and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Delhi?

The driest month is November and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is May at 30.4°C, and the coldest is January at 19.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Delhi?

Snow is rare in Delhi: the coldest average nights only drop to about 19.1°C, which is above freezing.
