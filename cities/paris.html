Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Paris?

Over the last ten years Paris averaged a daytime high of 16.4°C and a nighttime low of 8.2°C, with about 7733.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Paris?

The driest month is July and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 16.4°C, and the coldest is January at 8.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Paris?

Snow is rare in Paris: the coldest average nights only drop to about 8.2°C, which is above freezing.
