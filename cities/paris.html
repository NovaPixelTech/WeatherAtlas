Seasons in Paris

Paris is warmest in July, when average highs reach 25.4°C, and coolest in January at 7.3°C. Rainfall peaks in May (79.1 mm) and is lowest in July (45.7 mm).

May, June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Paris

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.4°C and about 45.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is May.

How Paris compares

With an annual mean of 12.3°C and about 773.3 mm of rain, Paris has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Paris?

Over the last ten years Paris averaged a daytime high of 16.4°C and a nighttime low of 8.2°C, with about 773.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Paris?

The driest month is July and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 25.4°C, and the coolest is January at 7.3°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Paris?

Snow is rare in Paris: even the coldest average nights stay around 1.8°C, above freezing.
