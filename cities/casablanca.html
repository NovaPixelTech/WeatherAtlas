Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Casablanca?

Over the last ten years Casablanca averaged a daytime high of 22.6°C and a nighttime low of 14.6°C, with about 3104.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Casablanca?

The driest month is July and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is August at 22.6°C, and the coldest is January at 14.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Casablanca?

Snow is rare in Casablanca: the coldest average nights only drop to about 14.6°C, which is above freezing.
