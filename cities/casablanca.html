Seasons in Casablanca

Casablanca is warmest in August, when average highs reach 27.7°C, and coolest in January at 17.5°C. Rainfall peaks in March (53.9 mm) and is lowest in July (0.8 mm).

March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Casablanca

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.1°C and about 0.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is March.

How Casablanca compares

With an annual mean of 18.6°C and about 310.5 mm of rain, Casablanca has generally mild to warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in March.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Casablanca?

Over the last ten years Casablanca averaged a daytime high of 22.6°C and a nighttime low of 14.6°C, with about 310.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Casablanca?

The driest month is July and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is August at 27.7°C, and the coolest is January at 17.5°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Casablanca?

Snow is rare in Casablanca: even the coldest average nights stay around 9°C, above freezing.
