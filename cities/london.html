Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in London?

Over the last ten years London averaged a daytime high of 15.1°C and a nighttime low of 7.4°C, with about 7209.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit London?

The driest month is April and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 15.1°C, and the coldest is January at 7.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in London?

Snow is rare in London: the coldest average nights only drop to about 7.4°C, which is above freezing.
