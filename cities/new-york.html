Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in New York?

Over the last ten years New York averaged a daytime high of 17°C and a nighttime low of 8.1°C, with about 12989.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit New York?

The driest month is November and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 17°C, and the coldest is January at 8.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in New York?

Snowfall is part of New York's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 8.1°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
