Seasons in Singapore

Singapore is warmest in March, when average highs reach 30.4°C, and coolest in January at 28.9°C. Rainfall peaks in November (382.2 mm) and is lowest in February (123.3 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Singapore

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 29.7°C and about 123.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is March and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is November.

How Singapore compares

With an annual mean of 27°C and about 3140 mm of rain, Singapore has consistently warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Singapore?

Over the last ten years Singapore averaged a daytime high of 29.5°C and a nighttime low of 24.5°C, with about 3140 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was March and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Singapore?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is March at 30.4°C, and the coolest is January at 28.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Singapore?

Snow is rare in Singapore: even the coldest average nights stay around 23.7°C, above freezing.
