Seasons in Warsaw

Warsaw is warmest in August, when average highs reach 25.3°C, and coolest in January at 2°C. Rainfall peaks in July (80.7 mm) and is lowest in March (39.6 mm).

May, June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Warsaw

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 20.2°C and about 61.2 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is July.

How Warsaw compares

With an annual mean of 10.1°C and about 696.2 mm of rain, Warsaw has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Warsaw?

Over the last ten years Warsaw averaged a daytime high of 14°C and a nighttime low of 6.2°C, with about 696.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Warsaw?

The driest month is March and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 25.3°C, and the coolest is January at 2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Warsaw?

Snow is possible in Warsaw: the coldest average nights drop to around -2.9°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
