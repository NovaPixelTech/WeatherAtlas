Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Warsaw?

Over the last ten years Warsaw averaged a daytime high of 14°C and a nighttime low of 6.2°C, with about 6961.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Warsaw?

The driest month is March and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 14°C, and the coldest is January at 6.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Warsaw?

Snowfall is part of Warsaw's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 6.2°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
