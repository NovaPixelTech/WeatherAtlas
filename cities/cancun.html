Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Cancún?

Over the last ten years Cancún averaged a daytime high of 28.9°C and a nighttime low of 24.1°C, with about 12517.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Cancún?

The driest month is March and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 28.9°C, and the coldest is January at 24.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Cancún?

Snow is rare in Cancún: the coldest average nights only drop to about 24.1°C, which is above freezing.
