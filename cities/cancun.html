Seasons in Cancún

Cancún is warmest in August, when average highs reach 31°C, and coolest in January at 26.1°C. Rainfall peaks in June (197.8 mm) and is lowest in March (36.7 mm).

January, February, March, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Cancún

Based on long-term averages, March is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 28°C and about 36.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is June.

How Cancún compares

With an annual mean of 26.5°C and about 1251.7 mm of rain, Cancún has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on March, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Cancún?

Over the last ten years Cancún averaged a daytime high of 28.9°C and a nighttime low of 24.1°C, with about 1251.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Cancún?

The driest month is March and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 31°C, and the coolest is January at 26.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Cancún?

Snow is rare in Cancún: even the coldest average nights stay around 21.6°C, above freezing.
