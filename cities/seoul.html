Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Seoul?

Over the last ten years Seoul averaged a daytime high of 16.9°C and a nighttime low of 7.6°C, with about 12358.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Seoul?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 16.9°C, and the coldest is January at 7.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Seoul?

Snowfall is part of Seoul's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 7.6°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
