Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Birmingham?

Over the last ten years Birmingham averaged a daytime high of 13.5°C and a nighttime low of 6.6°C, with about 8148.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Birmingham?

The driest month is April and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 13.5°C, and the coldest is January at 6.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Birmingham?

Snow is rare in Birmingham: the coldest average nights only drop to about 6.6°C, which is above freezing.
