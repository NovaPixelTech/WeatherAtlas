Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Barcelona?

Over the last ten years Barcelona averaged a daytime high of 20.5°C and a nighttime low of 12.5°C, with about 6346.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Barcelona?

The driest month is December and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 20.5°C, and the coldest is January at 12.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Barcelona?

Snow is rare in Barcelona: the coldest average nights only drop to about 12.5°C, which is above freezing.
