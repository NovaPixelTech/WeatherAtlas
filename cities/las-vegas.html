Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Las Vegas?

Over the last ten years Las Vegas averaged a daytime high of 27.2°C and a nighttime low of 14.8°C, with about 1229.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Las Vegas?

The driest month is June and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is July at 27.2°C, and the coldest is December at 14.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Las Vegas?

Snow is rare in Las Vegas: the coldest average nights only drop to about 14.8°C, which is above freezing.
