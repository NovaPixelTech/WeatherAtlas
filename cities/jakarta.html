Seasons in Jakarta

Jakarta is warmest in September, when average highs reach 32.6°C, and coolest in February at 29.5°C. Rainfall peaks in February (321.7 mm) and is lowest in August (58.7 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Jakarta

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 32.4°C and about 58.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is September and the coolest is February; the driest month is August and the wettest is February.

How Jakarta compares

With an annual mean of 27.5°C and about 2270.9 mm of rain, Jakarta has consistently warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in February.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Jakarta?

Over the last ten years Jakarta averaged a daytime high of 31.2°C and a nighttime low of 23.7°C, with about 2270.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was February.

When is the best time of year to visit Jakarta?

The driest month is August and the wettest is February. The hottest average high is September at 32.6°C, and the coolest is February at 29.5°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Jakarta?

Snow is rare in Jakarta: even the coldest average nights stay around 23.6°C, above freezing.
