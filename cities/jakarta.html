Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Jakarta?

Over the last ten years Jakarta averaged a daytime high of 31.2°C and a nighttime low of 23.7°C, with about 22709.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was February.

When is the best time of year to visit Jakarta?

The driest month is August and the wettest is February. The hottest average high is September at 31.2°C, and the coldest is February at 23.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Jakarta?

Snow is rare in Jakarta: the coldest average nights only drop to about 23.7°C, which is above freezing.
