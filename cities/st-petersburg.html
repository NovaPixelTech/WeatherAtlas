Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in St Petersburg?

Over the last ten years St Petersburg averaged a daytime high of 9.2°C and a nighttime low of 3.1°C, with about 7451.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit St Petersburg?

The driest month is March and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is July at 9.2°C, and the coldest is January at 3.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in St Petersburg?

Snowfall is part of St Petersburg's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 3.1°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
