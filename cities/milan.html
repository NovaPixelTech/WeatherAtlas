Seasons in Milan

Milan is warmest in July, when average highs reach 29.9°C, and coolest in January at 7.8°C. Rainfall peaks in May (163.4 mm) and is lowest in January (49.5 mm).

April, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Milan

Based on long-term averages, April is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18.5°C and about 87.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is May.

How Milan compares

With an annual mean of 14.2°C and about 1217.5 mm of rain, Milan has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on April, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Milan?

Over the last ten years Milan averaged a daytime high of 18.7°C and a nighttime low of 9.6°C, with about 1217.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Milan?

The driest month is January and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 29.9°C, and the coolest is January at 7.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Milan?

Snow is possible in Milan: the coldest average nights drop to around -0.7°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
