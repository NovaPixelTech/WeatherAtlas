Seasons in Kyiv

Kyiv is warmest in August, when average highs reach 26.5°C, and coolest in January at -0.2°C. Rainfall peaks in July (69.3 mm) and is lowest in September (35.2 mm).

May, June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Kyiv

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 21°C and about 35.2 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is September and the wettest is July.

How Kyiv compares

With an annual mean of 9.8°C and about 614.9 mm of rain, Kyiv has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Kyiv?

Over the last ten years Kyiv averaged a daytime high of 13.7°C and a nighttime low of 5.9°C, with about 614.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Kyiv?

The driest month is September and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 26.5°C, and the coolest is January at -0.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Kyiv?

Snow is possible in Kyiv: the coldest average nights drop to around -5.2°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
