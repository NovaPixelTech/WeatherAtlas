Seasons in Busan

Busan is warmest in August, when average highs reach 29°C, and coolest in January at 6.9°C. Rainfall peaks in July (269.1 mm) and is lowest in December (35.2 mm).

May, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Busan

Based on long-term averages, October is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 20.6°C and about 92.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is December and the wettest is July.

How Busan compares

With an annual mean of 14.5°C and about 1538.8 mm of rain, Busan has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on October, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Busan?

Over the last ten years Busan averaged a daytime high of 18.1°C and a nighttime low of 10.9°C, with about 1538.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Busan?

The driest month is December and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 29°C, and the coolest is January at 6.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Busan?

Snow is possible in Busan: the coldest average nights drop to around -1.7°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
