Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Los Angeles?

Over the last ten years Los Angeles averaged a daytime high of 25.3°C and a nighttime low of 12.6°C, with about 4037.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Los Angeles?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 25.3°C, and the coldest is January at 12.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Los Angeles?

Snow is rare in Los Angeles: the coldest average nights only drop to about 12.6°C, which is above freezing.
