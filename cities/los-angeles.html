Seasons in Los Angeles

Los Angeles is warmest in August, when average highs reach 32°C, and coolest in January at 18.9°C. Rainfall peaks in January (89.3 mm) and is lowest in July (2.5 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Los Angeles

Based on long-term averages, May is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 24.2°C and about 11 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How Los Angeles compares

With an annual mean of 19°C and about 403.7 mm of rain, Los Angeles has generally mild to warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on May, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Los Angeles?

Over the last ten years Los Angeles averaged a daytime high of 25.3°C and a nighttime low of 12.6°C, with about 403.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Los Angeles?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 32°C, and the coolest is January at 18.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Los Angeles?

Snow is rare in Los Angeles: even the coldest average nights stay around 7.7°C, above freezing.
