Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Munich?

Over the last ten years Munich averaged a daytime high of 14.4°C and a nighttime low of 5.5°C, with about 10639.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Munich?

The driest month is March and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 14.4°C, and the coldest is January at 5.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Munich?

Snowfall is part of Munich's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 5.5°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
