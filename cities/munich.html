Seasons in Munich

Munich is warmest in July, when average highs reach 24.3°C, and coolest in January at 4.1°C. Rainfall peaks in May (130.5 mm) and is lowest in March (61.3 mm).

June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Munich

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.4°C and about 93.1 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is May.

How Munich compares

With an annual mean of 10°C and about 1063.9 mm of rain, Munich has noticeably cool climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Munich?

Over the last ten years Munich averaged a daytime high of 14.4°C and a nighttime low of 5.5°C, with about 1063.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Munich?

The driest month is March and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 24.3°C, and the coolest is January at 4.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Munich?

Snow is possible in Munich: the coldest average nights drop to around -3°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
