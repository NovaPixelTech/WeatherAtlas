Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Shanghai?

Over the last ten years Shanghai averaged a daytime high of 21°C and a nighttime low of 13.5°C, with about 14766.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Shanghai?

The driest month is December and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is August at 21°C, and the coldest is January at 13.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Shanghai?

Snow is rare in Shanghai: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.5°C, which is above freezing.
