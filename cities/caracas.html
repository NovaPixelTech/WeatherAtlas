Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Caracas?

Over the last ten years Caracas averaged a daytime high of 27.2°C and a nighttime low of 18.7°C, with about 10386.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Caracas?

The driest month is January and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is September at 27.2°C, and the coldest is December at 18.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Caracas?

Snow is rare in Caracas: the coldest average nights only drop to about 18.7°C, which is above freezing.
