Seasons in Caracas

Caracas is warmest in September, when average highs reach 28.2°C, and coolest in December at 25.8°C. Rainfall peaks in November (152.4 mm) and is lowest in January (35.7 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, October, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Caracas

Based on long-term averages, January is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 26.1°C and about 35.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is September and the coolest is December; the driest month is January and the wettest is November.

How Caracas compares

With an annual mean of 23°C and about 1038.6 mm of rain, Caracas has generally mild to warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on January, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Caracas?

Over the last ten years Caracas averaged a daytime high of 27.2°C and a nighttime low of 18.7°C, with about 1038.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Caracas?

The driest month is January and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is September at 28.2°C, and the coolest is December at 25.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Caracas?

Snow is rare in Caracas: even the coldest average nights stay around 17.6°C, above freezing.
