Seasons in Marseille

Marseille is warmest in July, when average highs reach 28.4°C, and coolest in January at 11.4°C. Rainfall peaks in October (88.4 mm) and is lowest in July (5.9 mm).

May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Marseille

Based on long-term averages, June is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25.3°C and about 24.5 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is October.

How Marseille compares

With an annual mean of 15.8°C and about 567.7 mm of rain, Marseille has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on June, while those who do not mind rain can visit in October.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Marseille?

Over the last ten years Marseille averaged a daytime high of 19.3°C and a nighttime low of 12.3°C, with about 567.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Marseille?

The driest month is July and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 28.4°C, and the coolest is January at 11.4°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Marseille?

Snow is rare in Marseille: even the coldest average nights stay around 5.3°C, above freezing.
