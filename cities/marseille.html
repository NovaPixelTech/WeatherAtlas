Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Marseille?

Over the last ten years Marseille averaged a daytime high of 19.3°C and a nighttime low of 12.3°C, with about 5677.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Marseille?

The driest month is July and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 19.3°C, and the coldest is January at 12.3°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Marseille?

Snow is rare in Marseille: the coldest average nights only drop to about 12.3°C, which is above freezing.
