Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Auckland?

Over the last ten years Auckland averaged a daytime high of 18.4°C and a nighttime low of 13.4°C, with about 12051.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was February and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Auckland?

The driest month is December and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is February at 18.4°C, and the coldest is July at 13.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Auckland?

Snow is rare in Auckland: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.4°C, which is above freezing.
