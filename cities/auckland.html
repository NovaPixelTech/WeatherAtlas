Seasons in Auckland

Auckland is warmest in February, when average highs reach 23.1°C, and coolest in July at 14.3°C. Rainfall peaks in July (117.3 mm) and is lowest in December (86 mm).

January, February, March, April, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Auckland

Based on long-term averages, December is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 21.2°C and about 86 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is February and the coolest is July; the driest month is December and the wettest is July.

How Auckland compares

With an annual mean of 15.9°C and about 1205.1 mm of rain, Auckland has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on December, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Auckland?

Over the last ten years Auckland averaged a daytime high of 18.4°C and a nighttime low of 13.4°C, with about 1205.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was February and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Auckland?

The driest month is December and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is February at 23.1°C, and the coolest is July at 14.3°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Auckland?

Snow is rare in Auckland: even the coldest average nights stay around 9.7°C, above freezing.
