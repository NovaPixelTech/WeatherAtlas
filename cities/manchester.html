Seasons in Manchester

Manchester is warmest in July, when average highs reach 20°C, and coolest in January at 7.1°C. Rainfall peaks in December (118.4 mm) and is lowest in April (63 mm).

June, July, August offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Manchester

Based on long-term averages, June is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18.9°C and about 88.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is April and the wettest is December.

How Manchester compares

With an annual mean of 10.2°C and about 1179.4 mm of rain, Manchester has noticeably cool climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on June, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Manchester?

Over the last ten years Manchester averaged a daytime high of 13.6°C and a nighttime low of 6.7°C, with about 1179.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Manchester?

The driest month is April and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 20°C, and the coolest is January at 7.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Manchester?

Snow is rare in Manchester: even the coldest average nights stay around 1.8°C, above freezing.
