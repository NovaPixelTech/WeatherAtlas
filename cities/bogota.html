Seasons in Bogotá

Bogotá is warmest in September, when average highs reach 19.7°C, and coolest in July at 17.8°C. Rainfall peaks in November (132 mm) and is lowest in August (23.8 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Bogotá

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18.9°C and about 23.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is September and the coolest is July; the driest month is August and the wettest is November.

How Bogotá compares

With an annual mean of 14.2°C and about 888.7 mm of rain, Bogotá has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bogotá?

Over the last ten years Bogotá averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 9.4°C, with about 888.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Bogotá?

The driest month is August and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is September at 19.7°C, and the coolest is July at 17.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bogotá?

Snow is rare in Bogotá: even the coldest average nights stay around 9.2°C, above freezing.
