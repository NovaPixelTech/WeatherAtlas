Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bogotá?

Over the last ten years Bogotá averaged a daytime high of 18.9°C and a nighttime low of 9.4°C, with about 8887.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was September and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Bogotá?

The driest month is August and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is September at 18.9°C, and the coldest is July at 9.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bogotá?

Snow is rare in Bogotá: the coldest average nights only drop to about 9.4°C, which is above freezing.
