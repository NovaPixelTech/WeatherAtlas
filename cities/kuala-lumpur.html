Seasons in Kuala Lumpur

Kuala Lumpur is warmest in March, when average highs reach 31.6°C, and coolest in November at 29.8°C. Rainfall peaks in November (380.5 mm) and is lowest in February (117.3 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Kuala Lumpur

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 31.4°C and about 117.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is March and the coolest is November; the driest month is February and the wettest is November.

How Kuala Lumpur compares

With an annual mean of 27.3°C and about 2939.1 mm of rain, Kuala Lumpur has consistently warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Kuala Lumpur?

Over the last ten years Kuala Lumpur averaged a daytime high of 30.7°C and a nighttime low of 23.8°C, with about 2939.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was March and the coldest was November.

When is the best time of year to visit Kuala Lumpur?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is March at 31.6°C, and the coolest is November at 29.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur?

Snow is rare in Kuala Lumpur: even the coldest average nights stay around 23.6°C, above freezing.
