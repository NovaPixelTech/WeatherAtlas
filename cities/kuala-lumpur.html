Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Kuala Lumpur?

Over the last ten years Kuala Lumpur averaged a daytime high of 30.7°C and a nighttime low of 23.8°C, with about 29390.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was March and the coldest was November.

When is the best time of year to visit Kuala Lumpur?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is March at 30.7°C, and the coldest is November at 23.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Kuala Lumpur?

Snow is rare in Kuala Lumpur: the coldest average nights only drop to about 23.8°C, which is above freezing.
