Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Almaty?

Over the last ten years Almaty averaged a daytime high of 15.3°C and a nighttime low of 5.4°C, with about 6617.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Almaty?

The driest month is January and the wettest is April. The hottest average high is July at 15.3°C, and the coldest is January at 5.4°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Almaty?

Snowfall is part of Almaty's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 5.4°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
