Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tehran?

Over the last ten years Tehran averaged a daytime high of 23.5°C and a nighttime low of 12.3°C, with about 3046.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tehran?

The driest month is August and the wettest is March. The hottest average high is July at 23.5°C, and the coldest is January at 12.3°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tehran?

Snowfall is part of Tehran's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 12.3°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
