Seasons in Tel Aviv

Tel Aviv is warmest in August, when average highs reach 31.6°C, and coolest in January at 17.1°C. Rainfall peaks in January (130.9 mm) and is lowest in July (0.9 mm).

February, March, April, May, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Tel Aviv

Based on long-term averages, May is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.1°C and about 7.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How Tel Aviv compares

With an annual mean of 20.9°C and about 502.6 mm of rain, Tel Aviv has generally mild to warm climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on May, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tel Aviv?

Over the last ten years Tel Aviv averaged a daytime high of 25.1°C and a nighttime low of 16.7°C, with about 502.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tel Aviv?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 31.6°C, and the coolest is January at 17.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tel Aviv?

Snow is rare in Tel Aviv: even the coldest average nights stay around 9.7°C, above freezing.
