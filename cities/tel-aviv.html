Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Tel Aviv?

Over the last ten years Tel Aviv averaged a daytime high of 25.1°C and a nighttime low of 16.7°C, with about 5026.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Tel Aviv?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 25.1°C, and the coldest is January at 16.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Tel Aviv?

Snow is rare in Tel Aviv: the coldest average nights only drop to about 16.7°C, which is above freezing.
