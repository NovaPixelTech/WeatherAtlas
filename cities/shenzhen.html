Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Shenzhen?

Over the last ten years Shenzhen averaged a daytime high of 26.5°C and a nighttime low of 20°C, with about 19177.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Shenzhen?

The driest month is December and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is July at 26.5°C, and the coldest is January at 20°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Shenzhen?

Snow is rare in Shenzhen: the coldest average nights only drop to about 20°C, which is above freezing.
