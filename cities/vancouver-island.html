Seasons in Vancouver Island

Vancouver Island is warmest in August, when average highs reach 20.1°C, and coolest in December at -0.2°C. Rainfall peaks in January (418.8 mm) and is lowest in July (50.8 mm).

July, August offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Vancouver Island

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.2°C and about 50.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is December; the driest month is July and the wettest is January.

How Vancouver Island compares

With an annual mean of 4.5°C and about 2412 mm of rain, Vancouver Island has noticeably cool climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Vancouver Island?

Over the last ten years Vancouver Island averaged a daytime high of 8.3°C and a nighttime low of 0.7°C, with about 2412 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Vancouver Island?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 20.1°C, and the coolest is December at -0.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Vancouver Island?

Snow is possible in Vancouver Island: the coldest average nights drop to around -4.9°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
