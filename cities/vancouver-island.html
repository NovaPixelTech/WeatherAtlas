Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Vancouver Island?

Over the last ten years Vancouver Island averaged a daytime high of 8.3°C and a nighttime low of 0.7°C, with about 24119.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Vancouver Island?

The driest month is July and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is August at 8.3°C, and the coldest is December at 0.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Vancouver Island?

Snowfall is part of Vancouver Island's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 0.7°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
