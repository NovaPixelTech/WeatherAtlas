Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Johannesburg?

Over the last ten years Johannesburg averaged a daytime high of 22.9°C and a nighttime low of 10.2°C, with about 6062.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was October and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit Johannesburg?

The driest month is August and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is October at 22.9°C, and the coldest is June at 10.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Johannesburg?

Snow is rare in Johannesburg: the coldest average nights only drop to about 10.2°C, which is above freezing.
