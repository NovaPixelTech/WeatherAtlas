Seasons in Johannesburg

Johannesburg is warmest in October, when average highs reach 26.1°C, and coolest in June at 17.1°C. Rainfall peaks in January (117 mm) and is lowest in August (2.5 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Johannesburg

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 21°C and about 2.5 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is October and the coolest is June; the driest month is August and the wettest is January.

How Johannesburg compares

With an annual mean of 16.5°C and about 606.2 mm of rain, Johannesburg has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in January.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Johannesburg?

Over the last ten years Johannesburg averaged a daytime high of 22.9°C and a nighttime low of 10.2°C, with about 606.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was October and the coldest was June.

When is the best time of year to visit Johannesburg?

The driest month is August and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is October at 26.1°C, and the coolest is June at 17.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Johannesburg?

Snow is rare in Johannesburg: even the coldest average nights stay around 4.1°C, above freezing.
