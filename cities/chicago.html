Seasons in Chicago

Chicago is warmest in July, when average highs reach 26.9°C, and coolest in January at 0.2°C. Rainfall peaks in July (151.8 mm) and is lowest in February (61.2 mm).

May, June, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Chicago

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 23.9°C and about 96.1 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is July.

How Chicago compares

With an annual mean of 10.5°C and about 1176.7 mm of rain, Chicago has noticeably cool climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Chicago?

Over the last ten years Chicago averaged a daytime high of 14.5°C and a nighttime low of 6.5°C, with about 1176.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Chicago?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 26.9°C, and the coolest is January at 0.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Chicago?

Snow is possible in Chicago: the coldest average nights drop to around -6.9°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
