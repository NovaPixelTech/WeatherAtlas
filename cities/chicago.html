Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Chicago?

Over the last ten years Chicago averaged a daytime high of 14.5°C and a nighttime low of 6.5°C, with about 11766.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Chicago?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 14.5°C, and the coldest is January at 6.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Chicago?

Snowfall is part of Chicago's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 6.5°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
