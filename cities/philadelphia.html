Seasons in Philadelphia

Philadelphia is warmest in July, when average highs reach 30.9°C, and coolest in January at 5.4°C. Rainfall peaks in July (120 mm) and is lowest in November (76.4 mm).

May, June, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Philadelphia

Based on long-term averages, October is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.9°C and about 98.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is November and the wettest is July.

How Philadelphia compares

With an annual mean of 13.6°C and about 1233.2 mm of rain, Philadelphia has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on October, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Philadelphia?

Over the last ten years Philadelphia averaged a daytime high of 18.4°C and a nighttime low of 8.7°C, with about 1233.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Philadelphia?

The driest month is November and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 30.9°C, and the coolest is January at 5.4°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Philadelphia?

Snow is possible in Philadelphia: the coldest average nights drop to around -3°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
