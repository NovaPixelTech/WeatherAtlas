Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Philadelphia?

Over the last ten years Philadelphia averaged a daytime high of 18.4°C and a nighttime low of 8.7°C, with about 12332.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Philadelphia?

The driest month is November and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 18.4°C, and the coldest is January at 8.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Philadelphia?

Snowfall is part of Philadelphia's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 8.7°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
