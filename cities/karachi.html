Seasons in Karachi

Karachi is warmest in May, when average highs reach 33.5°C, and coolest in January at 23.9°C. Rainfall peaks in August (118.8 mm) and is lowest in November (0.9 mm).

January, February, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Karachi

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 26.9°C and about 1.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is May and the coolest is January; the driest month is November and the wettest is August.

How Karachi compares

With an annual mean of 26.5°C and about 248.1 mm of rain, Karachi has consistently warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in August.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Karachi?

Over the last ten years Karachi averaged a daytime high of 30.2°C and a nighttime low of 22.8°C, with about 248.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was May and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Karachi?

The driest month is November and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is May at 33.5°C, and the coolest is January at 23.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Karachi?

Snow is rare in Karachi: even the coldest average nights stay around 14.3°C, above freezing.
