Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Karachi?

Over the last ten years Karachi averaged a daytime high of 30.2°C and a nighttime low of 22.8°C, with about 2480.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was May and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Karachi?

The driest month is November and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is May at 30.2°C, and the coldest is January at 22.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Karachi?

Snow is rare in Karachi: the coldest average nights only drop to about 22.8°C, which is above freezing.
