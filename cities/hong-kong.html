Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Hong Kong?

Over the last ten years Hong Kong averaged a daytime high of 25.1°C and a nighttime low of 20.6°C, with about 22539.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Hong Kong?

The driest month is December and the wettest is August. The hottest average high is July at 25.1°C, and the coldest is January at 20.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Hong Kong?

Snow is rare in Hong Kong: the coldest average nights only drop to about 20.6°C, which is above freezing.
