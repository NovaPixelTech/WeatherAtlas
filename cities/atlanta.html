Seasons in Atlanta

Atlanta is warmest in July, when average highs reach 30.9°C, and coolest in January at 11.2°C. Rainfall peaks in July (178.7 mm) and is lowest in October (93.4 mm).

March, April, May, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Atlanta

Based on long-term averages, October is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.9°C and about 93.4 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is October and the wettest is July.

How Atlanta compares

With an annual mean of 17.1°C and about 1547.8 mm of rain, Atlanta has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on October, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Atlanta?

Over the last ten years Atlanta averaged a daytime high of 22.1°C and a nighttime low of 12.1°C, with about 1547.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Atlanta?

The driest month is October and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is July at 30.9°C, and the coolest is January at 11.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Atlanta?

Snow is rare in Atlanta: even the coldest average nights stay around 1.7°C, above freezing.
