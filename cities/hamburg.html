Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Hamburg?

Over the last ten years Hamburg averaged a daytime high of 14.1°C and a nighttime low of 6.8°C, with about 8141 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Hamburg?

The driest month is April and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 14.1°C, and the coldest is January at 6.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Hamburg?

Snow is rare in Hamburg: the coldest average nights only drop to about 6.8°C, which is above freezing.
