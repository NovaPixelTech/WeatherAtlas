Seasons in Hamburg

Hamburg is warmest in August, when average highs reach 23.4°C, and coolest in January at 4.9°C. Rainfall peaks in July (86.5 mm) and is lowest in April (50.6 mm).

May, June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Hamburg

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.6°C and about 57.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is April and the wettest is July.

How Hamburg compares

With an annual mean of 10.5°C and about 814.1 mm of rain, Hamburg has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Hamburg?

Over the last ten years Hamburg averaged a daytime high of 14.1°C and a nighttime low of 6.8°C, with about 814.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Hamburg?

The driest month is April and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 23.4°C, and the coolest is January at 4.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Hamburg?

Snow is rare in Hamburg: even the coldest average nights stay around 0.4°C, above freezing.
