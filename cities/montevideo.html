Seasons in Montevideo

Montevideo is warmest in January, when average highs reach 26.5°C, and coolest in July at 13.8°C. Rainfall peaks in April (110.2 mm) and is lowest in September (59.8 mm).

January, February, March, April, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Montevideo

Based on long-term averages, December is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 24.7°C and about 71.2 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is January and the coolest is July; the driest month is September and the wettest is April.

How Montevideo compares

With an annual mean of 17.2°C and about 973.8 mm of rain, Montevideo has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on December, while those who do not mind rain can visit in April.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Montevideo?

Over the last ten years Montevideo averaged a daytime high of 20.1°C and a nighttime low of 14.2°C, with about 973.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Montevideo?

The driest month is September and the wettest is April. The hottest average high is January at 26.5°C, and the coolest is July at 13.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Montevideo?

Snow is rare in Montevideo: even the coldest average nights stay around 8.6°C, above freezing.
