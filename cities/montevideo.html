Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Montevideo?

Over the last ten years Montevideo averaged a daytime high of 20.1°C and a nighttime low of 14.2°C, with about 9737.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was January and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Montevideo?

The driest month is September and the wettest is April. The hottest average high is January at 20.1°C, and the coldest is July at 14.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Montevideo?

Snow is rare in Montevideo: the coldest average nights only drop to about 14.2°C, which is above freezing.
