Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bangkok?

Over the last ten years Bangkok averaged a daytime high of 32.1°C and a nighttime low of 24.8°C, with about 14924.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was October.

When is the best time of year to visit Bangkok?

The driest month is December and the wettest is September. The hottest average high is April at 32.1°C, and the coldest is October at 24.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bangkok?

Snow is rare in Bangkok: the coldest average nights only drop to about 24.8°C, which is above freezing.
