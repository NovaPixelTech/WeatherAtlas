Seasons in Bangkok

Bangkok is warmest in April, when average highs reach 34.3°C, and coolest in October at 30.8°C. Rainfall peaks in September (293.3 mm) and is lowest in December (19.4 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Bangkok

Based on long-term averages, December is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 31°C and about 19.4 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is April and the coolest is October; the driest month is December and the wettest is September.

How Bangkok compares

With an annual mean of 28.5°C and about 1492.5 mm of rain, Bangkok has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on December, while those who do not mind rain can visit in September.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bangkok?

Over the last ten years Bangkok averaged a daytime high of 32.1°C and a nighttime low of 24.8°C, with about 1492.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was October.

When is the best time of year to visit Bangkok?

The driest month is December and the wettest is September. The hottest average high is April at 34.3°C, and the coolest is October at 30.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bangkok?

Snow is rare in Bangkok: even the coldest average nights stay around 24.4°C, above freezing.
