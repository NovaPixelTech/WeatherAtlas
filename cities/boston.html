Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Boston?

Over the last ten years Boston averaged a daytime high of 15.5°C and a nighttime low of 6.2°C, with about 11907.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Boston?

The driest month is August and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 15.5°C, and the coldest is January at 6.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Boston?

Snowfall is part of Boston's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 6.2°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
