Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Kolkata?

Over the last ten years Kolkata averaged a daytime high of 30.8°C and a nighttime low of 22.3°C, with about 18627.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Kolkata?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 30.8°C, and the coldest is January at 22.3°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Kolkata?

Snow is rare in Kolkata: the coldest average nights only drop to about 22.3°C, which is above freezing.
