Seasons in Kolkata

Kolkata is warmest in April, when average highs reach 35.5°C, and coolest in January at 25°C. Rainfall peaks in July (401 mm) and is lowest in January (18.7 mm).

January, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Kolkata

Based on long-term averages, January is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 25°C and about 18.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is April and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is July.

How Kolkata compares

With an annual mean of 26.6°C and about 1862.8 mm of rain, Kolkata has consistently warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on January, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Kolkata?

Over the last ten years Kolkata averaged a daytime high of 30.8°C and a nighttime low of 22.3°C, with about 1862.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Kolkata?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 35.5°C, and the coolest is January at 25°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Kolkata?

Snow is rare in Kolkata: even the coldest average nights stay around 13.6°C, above freezing.
