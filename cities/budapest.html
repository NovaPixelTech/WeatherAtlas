Seasons in Budapest

Budapest is warmest in July, when average highs reach 28.4°C, and coolest in January at 4.1°C. Rainfall peaks in May (79.3 mm) and is lowest in March (41.1 mm).

May, June, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Budapest

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.7°C and about 67.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is May.

How Budapest compares

With an annual mean of 12.1°C and about 676.4 mm of rain, Budapest has moderate climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Budapest?

Over the last ten years Budapest averaged a daytime high of 16.6°C and a nighttime low of 7.6°C, with about 676.4 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Budapest?

The driest month is March and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 28.4°C, and the coolest is January at 4.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Budapest?

Snow is possible in Budapest: the coldest average nights drop to around -2.5°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
