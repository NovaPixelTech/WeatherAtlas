Seasons in Honolulu

Honolulu is warmest in August, when average highs reach 29.1°C, and coolest in February at 24.7°C. Rainfall peaks in December (55.4 mm) and is lowest in July (20.1 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, June, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Honolulu

Based on long-term averages, June is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 28°C and about 25.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is February; the driest month is July and the wettest is December.

How Honolulu compares

With an annual mean of 24.6°C and about 462.7 mm of rain, Honolulu has consistently warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on June, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Honolulu?

Over the last ten years Honolulu averaged a daytime high of 27°C and a nighttime low of 22.1°C, with about 462.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was February.

When is the best time of year to visit Honolulu?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is August at 29.1°C, and the coolest is February at 24.7°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Honolulu?

Snow is rare in Honolulu: even the coldest average nights stay around 20°C, above freezing.
