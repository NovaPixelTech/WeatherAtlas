Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Honolulu?

Over the last ten years Honolulu averaged a daytime high of 27°C and a nighttime low of 22.1°C, with about 4626.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was February.

When is the best time of year to visit Honolulu?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is August at 27°C, and the coldest is February at 22.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Honolulu?

Snow is rare in Honolulu: the coldest average nights only drop to about 22.1°C, which is above freezing.
