Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Athens?

Over the last ten years Athens averaged a daytime high of 22.4°C and a nighttime low of 13.8°C, with about 4919.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Athens?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 22.4°C, and the coldest is January at 13.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Athens?

Snow is rare in Athens: the coldest average nights only drop to about 13.8°C, which is above freezing.
