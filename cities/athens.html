Seasons in Athens

Athens is warmest in July, when average highs reach 33.5°C, and coolest in January at 12.2°C. Rainfall peaks in December (86.4 mm) and is lowest in July (9.9 mm).

April, May, October, November offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Athens

Based on long-term averages, April is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 20.4°C and about 26.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is July and the wettest is December.

How Athens compares

With an annual mean of 18.1°C and about 491.9 mm of rain, Athens has generally mild to warm climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on April, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Athens?

Over the last ten years Athens averaged a daytime high of 22.4°C and a nighttime low of 13.8°C, with about 491.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Athens?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 33.5°C, and the coolest is January at 12.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Athens?

Snow is rare in Athens: even the coldest average nights stay around 5.1°C, above freezing.
