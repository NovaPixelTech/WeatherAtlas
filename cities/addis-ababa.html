Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Addis Ababa?

Over the last ten years Addis Ababa averaged a daytime high of 21.1°C and a nighttime low of 9.6°C, with about 14266.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was March and the coldest was August.

When is the best time of year to visit Addis Ababa?

The driest month is December and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is March at 21.1°C, and the coldest is August at 9.6°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Addis Ababa?

Snow is rare in Addis Ababa: the coldest average nights only drop to about 9.6°C, which is above freezing.
