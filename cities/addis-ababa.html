Seasons in Addis Ababa

Addis Ababa is warmest in March, when average highs reach 23.2°C, and coolest in August at 19.1°C. Rainfall peaks in July (345.4 mm) and is lowest in December (1.8 mm).

January, February, March, April, May, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Addis Ababa

Based on long-term averages, December is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 21.2°C and about 1.8 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is March and the coolest is August; the driest month is December and the wettest is July.

How Addis Ababa compares

With an annual mean of 15.4°C and about 1426.7 mm of rain, Addis Ababa has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on December, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Addis Ababa?

Over the last ten years Addis Ababa averaged a daytime high of 21.1°C and a nighttime low of 9.6°C, with about 1426.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was March and the coldest was August.

When is the best time of year to visit Addis Ababa?

The driest month is December and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is March at 23.2°C, and the coolest is August at 19.1°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Addis Ababa?

Snow is rare in Addis Ababa: even the coldest average nights stay around 10.3°C, above freezing.
