Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bengaluru?

Over the last ten years Bengaluru averaged a daytime high of 28.7°C and a nighttime low of 18.9°C, with about 10601.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Bengaluru?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 28.7°C, and the coldest is December at 18.9°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bengaluru?

Snow is rare in Bengaluru: the coldest average nights only drop to about 18.9°C, which is above freezing.
