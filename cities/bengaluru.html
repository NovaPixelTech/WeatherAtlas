Seasons in Bengaluru

Bengaluru is warmest in April, when average highs reach 33.7°C, and coolest in December at 26.3°C. Rainfall peaks in July (164.3 mm) and is lowest in February (3.6 mm).

January, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Bengaluru

Based on long-term averages, January is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.4°C and about 12.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is April and the coolest is December; the driest month is February and the wettest is July.

How Bengaluru compares

With an annual mean of 23.8°C and about 1060.2 mm of rain, Bengaluru has generally mild to warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on January, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Bengaluru?

Over the last ten years Bengaluru averaged a daytime high of 28.7°C and a nighttime low of 18.9°C, with about 1060.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Bengaluru?

The driest month is February and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 33.7°C, and the coolest is December at 26.3°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Bengaluru?

Snow is rare in Bengaluru: even the coldest average nights stay around 16.7°C, above freezing.
