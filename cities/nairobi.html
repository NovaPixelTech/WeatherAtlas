Seasons in Nairobi

Nairobi is warmest in February, when average highs reach 27.4°C, and coolest in July at 22.7°C. Rainfall peaks in April (186 mm) and is lowest in July (19.9 mm).

January, February, March, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Nairobi

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 22.7°C and about 19.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is February and the coolest is July; the driest month is July and the wettest is April.

How Nairobi compares

With an annual mean of 19.6°C and about 899.9 mm of rain, Nairobi has generally mild to warm climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in April.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Nairobi?

Over the last ten years Nairobi averaged a daytime high of 24.9°C and a nighttime low of 14.3°C, with about 899.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was February and the coldest was July.

When is the best time of year to visit Nairobi?

The driest month is July and the wettest is April. The hottest average high is February at 27.4°C, and the coolest is July at 22.7°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Nairobi?

Snow is rare in Nairobi: even the coldest average nights stay around 12.4°C, above freezing.
