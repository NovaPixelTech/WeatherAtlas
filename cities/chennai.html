Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Chennai?

Over the last ten years Chennai averaged a daytime high of 31.7°C and a nighttime low of 25.1°C, with about 13111.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was June and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Chennai?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is June at 31.7°C, and the coldest is January at 25.1°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Chennai?

Snow is rare in Chennai: the coldest average nights only drop to about 25.1°C, which is above freezing.
