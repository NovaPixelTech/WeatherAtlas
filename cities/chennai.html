Seasons in Chennai

Chennai is warmest in June, when average highs reach 35.2°C, and coolest in January at 28°C. Rainfall peaks in November (355.7 mm) and is lowest in February (4.9 mm).

January offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Chennai

Based on long-term averages, January is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 28°C and about 27.6 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is June and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is November.

How Chennai compares

With an annual mean of 28.4°C and about 1311.2 mm of rain, Chennai has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on January, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Chennai?

Over the last ten years Chennai averaged a daytime high of 31.7°C and a nighttime low of 25.1°C, with about 1311.2 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was June and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Chennai?

The driest month is February and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is June at 35.2°C, and the coolest is January at 28°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Chennai?

Snow is rare in Chennai: even the coldest average nights stay around 21.9°C, above freezing.
