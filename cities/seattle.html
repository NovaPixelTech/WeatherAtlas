Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Seattle?

Over the last ten years Seattle averaged a daytime high of 15.3°C and a nighttime low of 7.8°C, with about 12028.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Seattle?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is August at 15.3°C, and the coldest is December at 7.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Seattle?

Snow is rare in Seattle: the coldest average nights only drop to about 7.8°C, which is above freezing.
