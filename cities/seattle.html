Seasons in Seattle

Seattle is warmest in August, when average highs reach 24.8°C, and coolest in December at 7.4°C. Rainfall peaks in December (188.7 mm) and is lowest in July (14.3 mm).

June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Seattle

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 24.6°C and about 14.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is December; the driest month is July and the wettest is December.

How Seattle compares

With an annual mean of 11.6°C and about 1202.9 mm of rain, Seattle has noticeably cool climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Seattle?

Over the last ten years Seattle averaged a daytime high of 15.3°C and a nighttime low of 7.8°C, with about 1202.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was December.

When is the best time of year to visit Seattle?

The driest month is July and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is August at 24.8°C, and the coolest is December at 7.4°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Seattle?

Snow is rare in Seattle: even the coldest average nights stay around 2.9°C, above freezing.
