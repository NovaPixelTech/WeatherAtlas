Seasons in Miami

Miami is warmest in July, when average highs reach 30.8°C, and coolest in January at 23.8°C. Rainfall peaks in June (221.8 mm) and is lowest in February (64 mm).

January, February, March, April, November, December offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Miami

Based on long-term averages, February is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 24.9°C and about 64 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is February and the wettest is June.

How Miami compares

With an annual mean of 25°C and about 1505.1 mm of rain, Miami has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on February, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Miami?

Over the last ten years Miami averaged a daytime high of 27.8°C and a nighttime low of 22.2°C, with about 1505.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Miami?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is July at 30.8°C, and the coolest is January at 23.8°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Miami?

Snow is rare in Miami: even the coldest average nights stay around 17.2°C, above freezing.
