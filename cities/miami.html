Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Miami?

Over the last ten years Miami averaged a daytime high of 27.8°C and a nighttime low of 22.2°C, with about 15051.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Miami?

The driest month is February and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is July at 27.8°C, and the coldest is January at 22.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Miami?

Snow is rare in Miami: the coldest average nights only drop to about 22.2°C, which is above freezing.
