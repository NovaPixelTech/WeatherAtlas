Seasons in Vienna

Vienna is warmest in July, when average highs reach 27.5°C, and coolest in January at 4.2°C. Rainfall peaks in September (84.3 mm) and is lowest in March (31.6 mm).

May, June, July, August, September offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Vienna

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.5°C and about 60.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is September.

How Vienna compares

With an annual mean of 11.8°C and about 658.1 mm of rain, Vienna has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in September.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Vienna?

Over the last ten years Vienna averaged a daytime high of 16°C and a nighttime low of 7.5°C, with about 658.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Vienna?

The driest month is March and the wettest is September. The hottest average high is July at 27.5°C, and the coolest is January at 4.2°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Vienna?

Snow is possible in Vienna: the coldest average nights drop to around -1.8°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
