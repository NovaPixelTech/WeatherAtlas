Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Vienna?

Over the last ten years Vienna averaged a daytime high of 16°C and a nighttime low of 7.5°C, with about 6580.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Vienna?

The driest month is March and the wettest is September. The hottest average high is July at 16°C, and the coldest is January at 7.5°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Vienna?

Snowfall is part of Vienna's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 7.5°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
