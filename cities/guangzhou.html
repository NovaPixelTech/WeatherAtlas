Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Guangzhou?

Over the last ten years Guangzhou averaged a daytime high of 26.9°C and a nighttime low of 19.8°C, with about 21979.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Guangzhou?

The driest month is December and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is July at 26.9°C, and the coldest is January at 19.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Guangzhou?

Snow is rare in Guangzhou: the coldest average nights only drop to about 19.8°C, which is above freezing.
