Seasons in Dublin

Dublin is warmest in July, when average highs reach 19.1°C, and coolest in January at 8.3°C. Rainfall peaks in December (94.7 mm) and is lowest in April (62.5 mm).

July, August offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Dublin

Based on long-term averages, July is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 19.1°C and about 78.9 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is April and the wettest is December.

How Dublin compares

With an annual mean of 10.7°C and about 922.3 mm of rain, Dublin has noticeably cool climate and a moderately rainy yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on July, while those who do not mind rain can visit in December.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Dublin?

Over the last ten years Dublin averaged a daytime high of 13.4°C and a nighttime low of 7.9°C, with about 922.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Dublin?

The driest month is April and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 19.1°C, and the coolest is January at 8.3°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Dublin?

Snow is rare in Dublin: even the coldest average nights stay around 3.7°C, above freezing.
