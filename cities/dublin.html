Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Dublin?

Over the last ten years Dublin averaged a daytime high of 13.4°C and a nighttime low of 7.9°C, with about 9223.3 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Dublin?

The driest month is April and the wettest is December. The hottest average high is July at 13.4°C, and the coldest is January at 7.9°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Dublin?

Snow is rare in Dublin: the coldest average nights only drop to about 7.9°C, which is above freezing.
