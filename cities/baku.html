Seasons in Baku

Baku is warmest in August, when average highs reach 31.1°C, and coolest in January at 7.7°C. Rainfall peaks in November (37.3 mm) and is lowest in August (7 mm).

May, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Baku

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 26.1°C and about 18.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is August and the wettest is November.

How Baku compares

With an annual mean of 15.9°C and about 278 mm of rain, Baku has moderate climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in November.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Baku?

Over the last ten years Baku averaged a daytime high of 18.8°C and a nighttime low of 12.9°C, with about 278 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Baku?

The driest month is August and the wettest is November. The hottest average high is August at 31.1°C, and the coolest is January at 7.7°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Baku?

Snow is rare in Baku: even the coldest average nights stay around 3.1°C, above freezing.
