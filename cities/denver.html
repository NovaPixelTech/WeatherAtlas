Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Denver?

Over the last ten years Denver averaged a daytime high of 17.8°C and a nighttime low of 2.3°C, with about 4436.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Denver?

The driest month is December and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 17.8°C, and the coldest is January at 2.3°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Denver?

Snowfall is part of Denver's climate: the coldest average nights drop to around 2.3°C, which is at or below freezing. In the coldest months of the year the city regularly sees snow.
