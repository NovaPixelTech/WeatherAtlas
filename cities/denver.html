Seasons in Denver

Denver is warmest in July, when average highs reach 31.4°C, and coolest in January at 4.9°C. Rainfall peaks in May (98.4 mm) and is lowest in December (15.9 mm).

May, September, October offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Denver

Based on long-term averages, September is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27.7°C and about 18.7 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is July and the coolest is January; the driest month is December and the wettest is May.

How Denver compares

With an annual mean of 10.1°C and about 443.7 mm of rain, Denver has noticeably cool climate and a dry yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on September, while those who do not mind rain can visit in May.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Denver?

Over the last ten years Denver averaged a daytime high of 17.8°C and a nighttime low of 2.3°C, with about 443.7 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Denver?

The driest month is December and the wettest is May. The hottest average high is July at 31.4°C, and the coolest is January at 4.9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Denver?

Snow is possible in Denver: the coldest average nights drop to around -9.5°C, which is at or below freezing. Frost and occasional snow can occur in the coldest months.
