Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Manila?

Over the last ten years Manila averaged a daytime high of 30.7°C and a nighttime low of 24.7°C, with about 21769 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Manila?

The driest month is March and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 30.7°C, and the coldest is January at 24.7°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Manila?

Snow is rare in Manila: the coldest average nights only drop to about 24.7°C, which is above freezing.
