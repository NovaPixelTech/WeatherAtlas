Seasons in Manila

Manila is warmest in April, when average highs reach 33.4°C, and coolest in January at 29°C. Rainfall peaks in July (402.7 mm) and is lowest in March (24.3 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Manila

Based on long-term averages, March is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 31.7°C and about 24.3 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is April and the coolest is January; the driest month is March and the wettest is July.

How Manila compares

With an annual mean of 27.7°C and about 2176.9 mm of rain, Manila has consistently warm climate and a very wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on March, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Manila?

Over the last ten years Manila averaged a daytime high of 30.7°C and a nighttime low of 24.7°C, with about 2176.9 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was April and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Manila?

The driest month is March and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is April at 33.4°C, and the coolest is January at 29°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Manila?

Snow is rare in Manila: even the coldest average nights stay around 22.8°C, above freezing.
