Seasons in Osaka

Osaka is warmest in August, when average highs reach 32.4°C, and coolest in January at 9°C. Rainfall peaks in July (252.2 mm) and is lowest in January (59.1 mm).

No month perfectly matches the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain, so the driest month is the safest default for outdoor plans. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Osaka

Based on long-term averages, April is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 18.9°C and about 156.1 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is August and the coolest is January; the driest month is January and the wettest is July.

How Osaka compares

With an annual mean of 16.3°C and about 1757.8 mm of rain, Osaka has moderate climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on April, while those who do not mind rain can visit in July.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Osaka?

Over the last ten years Osaka averaged a daytime high of 20.4°C and a nighttime low of 12.2°C, with about 1757.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was August and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Osaka?

The driest month is January and the wettest is July. The hottest average high is August at 32.4°C, and the coolest is January at 9°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Osaka?

Snow is rare in Osaka: even the coldest average nights stay around 1°C, above freezing.
