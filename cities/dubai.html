Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Dubai?

Over the last ten years Dubai averaged a daytime high of 34.7°C and a nighttime low of 22.2°C, with about 925.5 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Dubai?

The driest month is June and the wettest is January. The hottest average high is July at 34.7°C, and the coldest is January at 22.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Dubai?

Snow is rare in Dubai: the coldest average nights only drop to about 22.2°C, which is above freezing.
