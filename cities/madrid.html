Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Madrid?

Over the last ten years Madrid averaged a daytime high of 21.3°C and a nighttime low of 9.8°C, with about 4610.8 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was July and the coldest was January.

When is the best time of year to visit Madrid?

The driest month is July and the wettest is October. The hottest average high is July at 21.3°C, and the coldest is January at 9.8°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Madrid?

Snow is rare in Madrid: the coldest average nights only drop to about 9.8°C, which is above freezing.
