Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Lagos?

Over the last ten years Lagos averaged a daytime high of 29.4°C and a nighttime low of 25.2°C, with about 13186.1 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was February and the coldest was August.

When is the best time of year to visit Lagos?

The driest month is December and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is February at 29.4°C, and the coldest is August at 25.2°C, which makes the milder months the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Lagos?

Snow is rare in Lagos: the coldest average nights only drop to about 25.2°C, which is above freezing.
