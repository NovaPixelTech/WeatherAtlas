Seasons in Lagos

Lagos is warmest in February, when average highs reach 31.1°C, and coolest in August at 27°C. Rainfall peaks in June (267.3 mm) and is lowest in December (16.1 mm).

August offer the closest match to the typical 18–28°C comfort range with moderate rain. These figures are ten-year monthly averages from Open-Meteo, so individual years can differ.

Best time to visit Lagos

Based on long-term averages, August is usually the most comfortable month, with an average high of 27°C and about 117.1 mm of precipitation. The hottest month is February and the coolest is August; the driest month is December and the wettest is June.

How Lagos compares

With an annual mean of 27.3°C and about 1318.6 mm of rain, Lagos has consistently warm climate and a fairly wet yearly rainfall profile. Travellers who prefer warm, dry weather should focus on August, while those who do not mind rain can visit in June.

Frequently asked questions

What is the climate like in Lagos?

Over the last ten years Lagos averaged a daytime high of 29.4°C and a nighttime low of 25.2°C, with about 1318.6 mm of precipitation per year. The warmest month on average was February and the coldest was August.

When is the best time of year to visit Lagos?

The driest month is December and the wettest is June. The hottest average high is February at 31.1°C, and the coolest is August at 27°C, so the milder months are usually the most comfortable for outdoor plans.

Does it snow in Lagos?

Snow is rare in Lagos: even the coldest average nights stay around 24.1°C, above freezing.
